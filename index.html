🍎 How to Install on iOS Safari

Apple iOS Safari requires adding Progressive Web Apps manually via the share sheet menu:

1

Open in Safari Browser

Ensure you are viewing this page in official Apple Safari browser on iPhone or iPad.

2

Tap Share Button

Tap the 📤 Share icon located at the bottom center of your iPhone screen (or top right on iPad).

3

Select "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the Share Sheet menu options and tap ➕ Add to Home Screen.

4

Tap "Add"

Confirm by tapping Add in the top-right corner. The PWA icon will appear on your Home Screen!

💻 Android & Desktop Installation
🤖

Android (Google Chrome / Samsung Internet)

Tap the "⚡ Install App Now" button above, or open browser menu (⋮) and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".

💻

Desktop (Chrome / Microsoft Edge)

Click the Install Icon inside the browser address bar (right side), or tap the Install button on this page.
